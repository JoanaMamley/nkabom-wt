Terms of service

Last updated 27 November 2026

Nkabom is the online community of WomenTrust alumnae. These terms are the rules for using the site. By creating an account, you agree to them. How we handle your data is in the privacy notice.

Who runs Nkabom

Nkabom is run by the executives of the WomenTrust Graduate Association, who look after it as the site's admins.

Who can join

  • Nkabom is for WomenTrust alumnae.
  • The executives check each registration before it is approved, and may turn down one they cannot confirm.
  • Each person may have one account.

Your account

  • Give true details about yourself, and keep them up to date.
  • Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and tell the executives.
  • If you log in with Google, Google's own terms apply to your account with it too.

Using Nkabom

  • Treat other members with respect.
  • Use what you see about other members, such as their profile or the contact details shared through mentorship, only for the community and mentorship. Do not share it outside Nkabom.
  • Do not use Nkabom to harass anyone, to send advertising or spam, or to pretend to be someone else.

The constitution

Members also agree to the WomenTrust Graduate Association's constitution, which they read and accept on the site when they register, and again whenever it changes. The constitution sets the rules of membership; these terms cover only the use of the site.

Ending an account

  • You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time, as the privacy notice explains.
  • The executives may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms or the constitution.

A free service

Nkabom is free, and is offered as it is. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but cannot promise that it will always be available or free of mistakes.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the last change, and we will tell members about any important one.
