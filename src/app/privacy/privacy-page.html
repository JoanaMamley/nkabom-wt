Privacy notice

Last updated 31 October 2026

Nkabom is the online community of WomenTrust alumnae. This notice explains what we keep about you, who can see it, and what you can do about it. It follows Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

When you register, we ask for:

  • your first name(s) and surname
  • your date of birth
  • whether you are a student or working, and your course of study or occupation
  • the year you graduated, or expect to graduate, from tertiary education
  • a profile photo

We also keep your email address, so you can log in and we can send you messages, and a record of the constitution version you accepted. If you use mentorship, we keep the requests you send and receive. If you turn on notifications, we keep a code for each device that receives them.

We do not use advertising or tracking, and we never sell your data.

Why we collect it

  • to check you are a WomenTrust alumna before your account is approved
  • to show your profile in the member directory, so alumnae can find mentors
  • so the executives can remember members' birthdays
  • to send you messages about your account, mentorship requests and changes to the constitution
  • to keep the community's records up to date

Who can see what

  • The executives, who run the site as its admins, can see your full record.
  • Other approved members can see only your directory profile: your first name, surname, photo, course or occupation, and graduation year. They never see your date of birth.
  • Your email address is shared with another member only when one of you accepts the other's mentorship request, and is hidden again when the mentorship ends.

Until your registration is approved, other members cannot see you at all.

Birthdays

Once you are approved, your name and birthday are added to a Google Calendar shared only with the executives, so they can remember your birthday. The calendar shows the day and month only: never the year you were born, your age, your photo or anything else about you. We never send you birthday messages from the site.

Where your data is kept

Nkabom runs on services from Google and Cloudflare. Google (Firebase) handles logging in, the database and notifications, keeps the birthday calendar, and sends our emails through Gmail. Cloudflare runs the site's server and stores photos and backups. Their servers may be outside Ghana. They store and process your data only to run the site for us.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your details while you are a member, and ask you once a year to check they are still right.
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your details, photo and birthday within 30 days.
  • If your registration is not approved, we delete it after 30 days.
  • Backup copies are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • We keep a short record of what the executives do on the site, such as approving or deleting an account, so the community's records can be checked.

Your rights

  • See your data: your profile shows everything you gave us.
  • Correct it: you can edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete it: you can ask for your account and data to be deleted, as above.
  • Stop mentorship requests: you can turn them off in your profile at any time.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.
